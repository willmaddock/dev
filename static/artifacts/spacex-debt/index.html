What are extraordinary measures?

Treasury can use bookkeeping steps to keep paying bills for a while after the limit is reached. How long that lasts is uncertain, so treat this slider as a what-if, not a prediction.

Why this is not a forecast. Borrowing is uneven (tax dates, interest payments), and the limit applies to debt subject to the limit, not exactly total debt. Analysts at the Bipartisan Policy Center expect the limit to be reached between late winter and mid-summer 2027. Extraordinary measures can delay that date, so a real deadline would fall later. The $8.3B figure is my own arithmetic from two rounded numbers ($40.0T on Aug 19 to $40.59T on Oct 29), so treat it as approximate.
Reported: the $41.1T limit, total debt of $40.59T on Oct 29 (Treasury) and $40T on Aug 19 (AP). My own estimates: the straight-line date, the Aug–Oct pace (computed from those two debt figures), and the Half and Double paces (half and twice the measured 1-year pace), which are illustrative.
