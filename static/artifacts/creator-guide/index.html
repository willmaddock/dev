    Prompt Studio · Section 3

    Copy into your AI platform

    Use Copy Full Operating Prompt in Studio, open a fresh AI chat, and paste the copied text as the first message. The notes you copied from this guide are not a replacement for the generated prompt.

    Handoff · Creator Guide → Studio

    This guide builds your custom menu and a Studio recipe, not the complete operating prompt. Copy your menu and enter it in the separate Prompt Studio. Studio remains the authoritative prompt generator.

    Your Studio setup recipe

      The menu text and recipe are instructions for the real Studio. They are not the full operating prompt.

      Prompt Studio · Generate

      Open Prompt Studio, choose Custom menu, enter your choices, and use Generate and Review Prompt. Copy the full operating prompt only after checking it.

      Your AI platform · Test

      Paste the full operating prompt into a new AI chat. Test choice selection, menu or 0 to return, and Exit to leave the role-play.

      Troubleshooting: My custom menu did not appear

      Confirm Custom menu mode, valid titles/descriptions, and that you regenerated the complete prompt in Studio. Your menu draft alone is insufficient.

      Your settings plan

      Save settings here as a checklist for manually configuring the separate Studio. They are not applied to Studio automatically.
