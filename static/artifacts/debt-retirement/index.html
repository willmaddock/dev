What this does not cover

The US has never missed a debt payment, so the prolonged-breach numbers come from model scenarios that predate today. The bond changes are my assumptions: in 2011 Treasury yields fell, and in a breach analysts expect them to spike, but I did not find a sourced size. Pensions, Social Security and income from dividends or rentals are not modeled, and I did not source whether payments could be delayed. This is not financial advice.
